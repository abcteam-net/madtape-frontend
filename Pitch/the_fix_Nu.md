# Madtape Website + Business Model Alignment Prompt

You are fixing the Madtape website and business model so it matches the strongest investor-ready positioning from the latest pitch deck.

## Core Strategic Decision

Madtape must NOT be positioned as “another AI video generation tool.”

Madtape is:

> The platform layer for AI-native short-form cinema.

The product is a publishing home, discovery feed, workflow-transparent learning layer, creator community, and audience-backed support system for AI-native cinematic shorts and episodes.

AI generation is only an optional add-on. It is not the core product.

## Brutal Business Rule

Do not make Madtape look like it competes directly with Runway, Pika, Dreamina, Seedance, or LTX as a generation engine.

That is a losing position because those companies own compute, model infrastructure, and capital-heavy generation workflows.

Madtape’s stronger wedge is:

1. Publishing
2. Discovery
3. Workflow transparency
4. Creator support
5. Audience validation
6. Optional generation tools only after the platform loop is clear

## Main Website Goal

Rewrite the website so a visitor understands this in 10 seconds:

## Business Model Rewrite

The website business model must use three phased revenue engines.

### Phase 1: Publishing Subscriptions

These are creator publishing plans, not unlimited compute plans.

Suggested structure:

#### Free

$0/month

* Publish up to 5 films per month
* Workflow pages
* Community access
* Public creator profile

#### Creator

$15/month

* Publish up to 20 films per month
* Creator badge
* Basic analytics
* Workflow library
* Priority visibility in selected community areas

#### Pro

$39/month

* Higher publishing capacity or unlimited publishing
* Advanced analytics
* Challenge priority
* Creator support tools
* Early access to campaign tools

Important:
“Unlimited” may only apply to publishing, not compute.

Do not use “unlimited video generation.”

### Phase 2: Direct Creator Support

Use this model:

* 95% to creator
* 5% Madtape platform fee
* Fees clearly disclosed
* Payment processing and taxes may apply
* No fake income promises
* No guaranteed earnings

Website copy:

> Madtape succeeds when creators build real audience support, not when they burn expensive compute.

### Phase 3: Optional Generation Add-ons

Keep Madness Credits, but reposition them.

Madness Credits are NOT the main business model.

They are only a compute-control layer for optional generation.

Use this structure:

> Madness Credits let creators pay for optional generation workflows based on real compute cost.

Supported workflows may include:

* Text-to-video
* Image-to-video
* Video-to-video
* Extend
* Upscale
* Retry
* Higher resolution renders

Critical rule:
Delete any fixed public claim like:

> $0.10/video

unless it is fully verified against real provider costs and still leaves margin.

Replace it with:

> Credit burn depends on model, duration, resolution, workflow type, and provider cost. The creator sees the estimated credit cost before rendering.

Pricing logic:

> Every generation workflow must be priced above raw provider cost with a clear margin buffer. No flat public generation price should be displayed unless the cost is verified and profitable.

## Unit Economics Guardrails

Add internal logic and public-facing copy around sustainability.

Public copy:

> Madtape avoids unlimited-compute traps. Publishing plans stay affordable because generation is optional and priced separately by real usage.

Internal product rule:

* Never bundle unlimited AI generation into creator subscriptions
* Never sell compute below provider cost
* Every render must show estimated credit burn before execution
* Failed generations, retries, refunds, and abuse must be accounted for
* Video-to-video and high-resolution workflows must burn more credits
* Provider costs must be editable from admin/backend settings

## FAQ Additions

### Is Madtape an AI video generator?

No. Madtape is a publishing and discovery platform for AI-native cinema. Optional generation tools may be available, but the core product is publishing, workflow transparency, audience discovery, and creator support.

### Why do films include workflows?

Because AI-native cinema is also a learning ecosystem. Viewers and creators can understand how a film was made, what tools were used, how long it took, and what it cost.

### How does creator support work?

Supporters can directly support creators. Creators receive 95% of the platform support split, while Madtape takes a clearly disclosed 5% platform fee. Payment processing fees and taxes may apply.

### Are Madness Credits required?

No. Madness Credits are only for optional generation workflows. Publishing and discovery are separate from compute usage.

### Is this crowdfunding?

Madtape is testing reward-based audience support for creative projects. It is not equity investment, profit sharing, or financial crowdfunding.
