# Madtape Website Rebuild Prompt for Antigravity

You are editing the existing Madtape website.

The goal is NOT to build a full streaming platform yet.

The goal is to convert the website into a traction-focused pre-launch landing experience for:

1. Viewers who want to watch AI short films.
2. AI filmmakers who want to submit their films.
3. Early supporters, accelerators, and investors who need to understand the market wedge quickly.

Madtape positioning:

Madtape is the home of short-form AI cinema.

It starts as a curated launchpad and early-access destination for cinematic AI short films, beginning with Rootsapiens / Omega Valley as the first flagship Madtape Original.

Do not position Madtape as a generic AI video generator.
Do not overbuild marketplace, credits, streaming subscriptions, or complex platform features yet.
Do not make the site feel like a SaaS dashboard.
Make it feel like a cinematic pre-launch platform, festival, and creator movement.

---

## Main Strategic Objective

Rebuild the website around three conversion goals:

1. Watch the first Madtape Original.
2. Join the early access waitlist.
3. Submit an AI short film.

Every page section must push toward one of these three actions.

## Design Direction

Use dark cinematic identity.

Visual tone:

- Premium
- Gritty
- Clean
- High contrast
- Short-form cinema
- AI-native but not childish
- Film festival energy, not generic tech startup

Avoid:

- Bright SaaS gradients
- Generic AI robot visuals
- Overloaded text blocks
- Fake platform metrics
- Fake testimonials
- “Netflix of AI” as the main headline

You may use “a launchpad for AI short films” and “the home of short-form AI cinema.”

---

## Copy Rules

Use short, direct copy.

Do not over-explain.

Every section should answer one of these:

- What is Madtape?
- Why should I care?
- What can I watch?
- How do I submit?
- How do I join?

Remove weak language like:

- revolutionizing
- unlocking the future
- next-generation ecosystem
- seamless experience
- cutting-edge platform

Use stronger language:

- AI films need a home.
- Short-form cinema is changing.
- Submit your AI short film.
- Watch the first Madtape Original.
- Join early access.
