## Slide 02 — The Problem

# Independent AI creators can produce faster than the market can support them.

AI video tools changed production speed, but the ecosystem is broken:

- Creators pay high generation costs before they know if an audience wants the story.
- AI films are scattered across YouTube, TikTok, Discord, and private links.
- Viewers can like content, but they cannot meaningfully help fund the next episode.
- Emerging filmmakers lack a serious platform built specifically for AI-native cinema.

**Core pain:** AI creators now have production power, but no structured economy around production, audience funding, distribution, and revenue sharing.

**Visual direction:** Split screen: creator burning credits alone vs. audience demand locked outside the production process.

## Slide 03 — The Opportunity

# AI cinema needs its own Netflix, Kickstarter, and creator economy layer.

Short-form AI cinema is becoming a new content category. It needs infrastructure built around how AI creators actually work:

- Fast production cycles
- Serialized stories
- Community-backed episodes
- Tokenized internal production credits
- Fan-powered validation before spending money
- Monetization through ads, subscriptions, and premium viewing

**Madtape is positioned as the platform layer for AI-native cinematic content.**

**Visual direction:** Three converging lanes into Madtape: Production, Funding, Streaming.

## Slide 04 — The Solution

Madtape is a platform where creators can:

**Revenue streams:**

1. Creator subscriptions  
   - Starter: $250/month

2. Viewer monetization  
   - Ad-supported viewing  
   - Ad-free subscription  
   - Premium episode access

3. Platform commission  
   - Commission on funded campaigns  
   - Commission on paid viewing and creator earnings

# 3-Minute Pitch Script

Hi, I’m Nadeem Issaid, founder of Madtape AI.

AI video tools have changed film production. Today, one creator can produce scenes that used to require a studio. But the business ecosystem around this new type of cinema is still broken.

AI creators are paying high costs for tools, credits, subscriptions, storage, and post-production before they know if anyone actually wants the story. Their work is scattered across YouTube, TikTok, Discord, and private links. Viewers can like a video, but they cannot meaningfully help the creator make the next episode.

Madtape solves this by connecting production, audience funding, streaming, and monetization in one platform for AI-native cinema.

Creators can pitch a film or series, build demand, receive audience support, publish episodes, and monetize through subscriptions, ads, premium access, and platform-supported campaigns. Viewers can discover AI films, follow creators, support upcoming episodes, and watch content through ad-supported or ad-free models.

The business model starts with creator subscriptions, viewer subscriptions, premium viewing, advertising, and platform commissions. We will be careful with any profit-sharing concept, because anything investment-like must be legally validated first. The first version focuses on support, rewards, subscriptions, and transparent campaign mechanics.

Our first beachhead is short-form AI series creators: AI filmmakers, film students, creative technologists, YouTube storytellers, and micro-studios. These people already produce, already pay for tools, and already need a better way to monetize and validate their work.

The next 90 days are about proof: onboard 20 creators, publish 50 AI shorts or episodes, reach 1,000 registered viewers, test five campaigns, and convert the first paying creators and supporters.

We are applying to Start it @KBC because Madtape needs serious pressure on its business model, legal structure, funding strategy, and go-to-market execution. Belgium can be the launch point for a European AI cinema platform.

Madtape is not another AI tool. It is the economy around AI cinema.

# Visual Identity Instructions for Design Agent

Use the existing Madtape website direction as the base.

**Tone:** cinematic, dark, premium, technological, creator-first.  
**Avoid:** childish AI gradients, generic startup icons, fake 3D SaaS dashboards, too many cards.  
**Use:** full-bleed cinematic stills, minimal typography, metallic logo treatment, subtle red/white contrast, dark backgrounds, strong visual hierarchy.  
**Typography:** bold condensed title style, clean readable body font.  
**Motion idea:** each section feels like a film trailer beat, not a corporate SaaS deck.  
**Slide density:** maximum 3 to 5 short lines per visual slide. Put detail in speaker notes.

# Brutal Fixes Before Submission

1. Do not overuse the word “token” in the application. It creates regulatory anxiety.
2. Do not say viewers “share profit” unless a lawyer structures it.
3. Say “support, rewards, credits, subscriptions, transparent revenue dashboards” first.
4. Present Madtape as a platform and marketplace, not only a website gallery.
5. Show one working demo or prototype screen before applying.
6. Prepare a 3-minute no-slide verbal pitch because Start it @KBC pitch day does not rely on slide decks.
7. Use hard validation targets. Without numbers, it sounds like an artistic idea, not a startup.
